local weather bridge for Wi-Fi weather stations

Your weather station's cloud, in your own home.

fauxcloud is free, open-source firmware (GPL-3.0-or-later). Your weather station uploads to it instead of the cloud. No soldering: you install it on an ESP32 board from your browser.

  • No internet needed. Nothing leaves the house.
  • Online services and airport weather, if you want them. Off by default.

Works with Weather Underground-compatible stations. Will it work with mine?

How it works

Works with Weather Underground-compatible stations.

If your console's setup page has a Weather Underground box, it may work (not tested yet; a console report tells us). If it pairs only through an app, it will not.

Vevor 7-in-1, model YT60234Verified

Consoles with the same setup page may work; consoles that pair only through an app or upload only over HTTPS will not.

See the full list, or report your console. The board it runs on: ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit.

What you need

  • An ESP32-WROOM-32 board4 MB flash, about €5. One console per board. Board notes

Troubleshooting

My console never got online

The console's setup Wi-Fi (AP) never appears, or the cloud says offline. fauxcloud gives the console its own network to upload to.
